Domino Game: Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

In short

  • We do not collect, send or sell any personal information about your child or you.
  • No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. We only count, anonymously, how often the game is opened (see "Counting how often the game is opened").
  • The Android app asks for internet only, for that count: no camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • Progress (pictures traced, stickers, the player's name) is saved only on your device.

Who we are

Domino Game is a free learning game for children from Class 1 to Class 7, made by Saiqul Modi. Children trace pictures, letters, numbers and maps, topple dominoes, hear and spell names in English, Hindi and Bengali, and learn road safety by playing.

What stays on the device

To remember a child's progress the game saves, on the device only:

  • the player name typed in the game (we suggest a first name or a nickname) and the chosen animal picture, for up to six players;
  • pictures traced, stickers, rainbow cap colours, quiz and spelling scores, safe road crossings, stories finished and the days played;
  • settings: language, colour theme and which guides were already shown.

This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android: Settings > Apps > Domino Game > Storage > Clear storage). In a web browser, clear this website's data.

Counting how often the game is opened

To know how many people play, we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that uses no cookies. When the Android app starts it sends one "app opened" message with the app version and the Android version. When the game or download page opens in a browser, GoatCounter counts the page visit with the browser type, screen size and country. Nothing about the child is sent: no name, progress, account or device id. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses; it only shows us totals, such as openings per day. If the device is offline, nothing is sent and the game plays the same. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Speaking names out loud

Names and instructions are spoken with the text-to-speech voice that is already on the device (for example Google Speech Services on Android). The game passes only the words to be spoken, such as "Cat. C, A, T", to that voice. It does not record or listen to anything: the game never uses the microphone.

"Watch the real one" videos

Some pictures have a red button that shows a short real-life video (for example a real tiger or a famous place in India). Every video was checked by us and comes from Wikimedia Commons or our own GitHub Pages website. The video opens in the device's web browser, outside the game; those websites have their own privacy policies. We recommend that a grown-up is nearby when a child watches.

Playing in a web browser

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages (saiqulmodi.github.io), and the Python engine that runs it is loaded from pygame-web.github.io. Like any website, these hosts may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. Apart from the anonymous page count above, the game sends nothing. Progress is kept in the browser's own storage, and a copy of the game is kept in the browser so it can open offline.

Children's privacy

The game is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the game contains no advertising and no links to social media. This policy is written to meet the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Please write to us through github.com/saiqulmodi/DominoGame/issues or the contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
